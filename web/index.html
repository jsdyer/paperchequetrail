How to read this ledger

Count share divides the number of cheques issued by all domestic payments for that department and product in the same month. Value share uses the Canadian-dollar amounts. A person can receive several payments. Delivery and cashing dates are outside this file.

A fiscal year begins in April. October is fiscal month 7. Codes keep similarly named products and departments separate. An absent row is shown as unavailable. A published zero is shown as zero.
